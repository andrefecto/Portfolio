André Fecteau

Assistant Director, Cloud Infrastructure Services
University of Colorado Boulder

Multicloud strategy, platform engineering, and organizational leadership.

I lead Cloud Foundation Services, Platform Engineering, and Virtual Infrastructure & Storage at CU Boulder. My teams drive strategy, product ownership, and service maturation across AWS, Azure, GCP, OpenShift, and VMware environments. The organization is new and the teams came from different places, so a big part of my role is helping things work together smoothly while keeping our platforms reliable and boring (in a good way).

I have a background in organizational leadership, so I care as much about how teams work as the technology itself. My goal is to reduce friction, create clarity, and build systems that support good work instead of getting in the way.

I started out in desktop support at the University of Connecticut, where I also earned my Individualized Bachelor of Arts in Information Systems Operations. I worked my way up from student worker to full-time staff before moving to Laramie in July 2019 to become a System Administrator at the University of Wyoming. While at UW, I grew into leadership roles in Business Intelligence and data, and in 2022 I completed my Master of Science in Organizational Leadership from Trine University. In July 2025, I took on my current role at CU Boulder, working remotely from Laramie.

Leadership Foundation

My leadership approach was shaped through graduate work in Organizational Leadership and refined over years of building and supporting technology teams. The philosophy, credo, code of ethics, and strengths below are the foundation I lean on when making decisions and developing people.

Leadership Philosophy

I learned early in my technology career that building great relationships with our customers comes down to doing the little things right. When working in desktop support, that meant going the extra mile. If you replaced someone's computer, you put their files back exactly where they were before. When you set up a new computer, you cable-managed their desk for them. As a Systems Specialist, doing the little things right meant being a good steward of the University of Wyoming's data. This quote is a standing reminder that every task we do for our customers is a chance to reinforce that our organization is here to help them, not get in the way of progress toward their mission or the university's.
